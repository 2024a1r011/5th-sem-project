Data Analyst

About the role
Join our analytics team to turn business data into insights that drive decisions.

Responsibilities
- Clean, transform and analyse large datasets using Python and SQL.
- Build dashboards and reports for business stakeholders.
- Develop simple predictive models to support forecasting.
- Present findings clearly to non-technical audiences.

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in a quantitative field.
- Proficiency in Python (pandas, NumPy) and SQL.
- Experience with a BI tool such as Power BI or Tableau.
- Understanding of statistics and basic machine learning.

Nice to have
- Experience with A/B testing.
