Backend Engineer (Junior)

About the role
We are looking for a Backend Engineer to join our platform team and help build the services behind our product.

Responsibilities
- Develop scalable backend services and work with REST APIs.
- Design and optimise relational database schemas and queries.
- Write unit and integration tests for the code you ship.
- Collaborate with frontend engineers and product managers.
- Deploy and monitor services using Docker and cloud platforms.

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field.
- Strong proficiency in Python (Flask, Django or FastAPI).
- Experience with SQL databases such as PostgreSQL or MySQL.
- Familiarity with Git, Docker and CI/CD pipelines.
- Good communication skills and ability to work in a team.

Nice to have
- Experience with caching (Redis) or message queues.
- Exposure to AWS, GCP or Azure.
